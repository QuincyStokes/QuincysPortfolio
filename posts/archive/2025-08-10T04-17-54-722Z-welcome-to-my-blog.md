# Welcome to My Blog!

Hello there! I'm Quincy Stokes, and I'm thrilled to finally have a blog where I can share my journey, thoughts, and experiences in game development and software engineering.

## Why a Blog?

After working on various projects and learning countless lessons along the way, I realized that sharing these experiences could be valuable not just for me, but for others who might be on similar paths. Whether you're:

- A fellow game developer
- Someone interested in software engineering
- Just curious about the creative process behind games
- Looking for insights into the industry

This blog is for you!

## What to Expect

I plan to write about:

### Game Development
- **Project insights** from my current games
- **Technical challenges** and how I solved them
- **Design decisions** and the reasoning behind them
- **Lessons learned** from failures and successes

### Industry Thoughts
- **Trends** I'm seeing in game development
- **Tools and technologies** that excite me
- **Career advice** based on my experiences
- **Industry observations** and predictions

### Life Updates
- **Personal projects** I'm working on
- **Learning goals** and achievements
- **Balancing work and life** as a developer
- **Random thoughts** and inspirations

## My Current Focus

Right now, I'm particularly excited about:

1. **GEODE** - My tower defense survival game that's been evolving
2. **Starlight Stray** - The atmospheric dungeon crawler from the game jam
3. **The Brilliant Detective Milo** - My narrative-driven cat detective game
4. **Learning new technologies** and expanding my skill set

## Daily Posts?

Yes, I'm aiming for daily posts! While that might sound ambitious, I believe that:

- **Consistency** is key to building good habits
- **Daily reflection** helps me process what I'm learning
- **Regular updates** keep me accountable to my goals
- **Frequent content** gives readers something to look forward to

## Let's Connect

I'd love to hear from you! Feel free to:

- **Comment** on posts (when I add that feature)
- **Reach out** through my contact page
- **Follow** my journey as it unfolds
- **Share** your own experiences and insights
